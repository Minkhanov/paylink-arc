PayLink is live on Arc mainnet, chain 5042. The Circle products it uses today are Arc and USDC.

Arc first. In web/config.js, lines 9 to 14, the app is pointed at Arc mainnet and its RPC, with the deployed contract address on line 13.

USDC is Arc's native token, and it is the payment asset. In src/PayLink.sol, the pay function on line 76 is payable. Line 83 checks that msg.value equals the invoice amount, so the payer needs only USDC: no approve step and no second token. Lines 110 and 111 forward the USDC to the merchant in the same call.

In web/app.js, line 18 sets Arc's 18 native decimals; lines 73 to 83 convert them to the six-decimal amounts people expect; line 228 registers Arc in the wallet with USDC as its currency; and lines 398 and 399 show the network fee in dollars.

Planned in this grant: Circle Bridge Kit for paying from any chain, in milestone one, and EURC, in milestone two. Neither is integrated yet.
